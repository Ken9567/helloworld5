## Entry 1

### Original

Today, my parents came to Tokyo and we met in my apartment. They arrived around 11 am, so we had lunch together. The lunch was sushi. We went to Hamazushi which is one the most famous sushi restaurant. My baby is 10 months old and cannot eat sushi, but I ordered Kake-Udon and cut small pieces so that she can eat. When my she ate the Udon, (顔を顰めた。). After finishing the lunch, we went to a baby shop close to the sushi restaurant. My parents some cloths and toys for my baby.

### Revised

Today, my parents came to Tokyo and visited my apartment. They arrived around 11 a.m., so we went out for sushi for lunch. We went to Hamazushi, one of the most famous conveyor-belt sushi chains. My daughter is 10 months old and can't eat sushi yet, so I ordered kake udon and cut it into small pieces so she could eat it. When she tasted the udon, she made a face. After lunch, we went to a baby store near the restaurant. My parents bought her some clothes and toys.

### Feedback

| Original | Revised | ポイント |
|---|---|---|
| we met in my apartment | (they) visited my apartment / came over to my place | 家に来たなら "visit" や "come over" が自然。"meet" だと待ち合わせの印象。場所を言うなら in より **at** my apartment |
| so we had lunch together. The lunch was sushi. | so we went out for sushi for lunch | 2文を1文にまとめるとすっきり。"go out for 〜" =「〜を食べに出かける」 |
| one the most famous sushi restaurant | one of the most famous ... chains | **one of the + 最上級 + 複数名詞**。"of" の抜けと複数形に注意 |
| Hamazushi which is ... | Hamazushi, one of ... | 固有名詞の補足説明はカンマで区切る(非制限用法)。"which is" は省略可 |
| sushi restaurant | conveyor-belt sushi chain | 回転寿司は "conveyor-belt sushi"。チェーン店なら "chain" |
| My baby ... cannot eat sushi | My daughter ... can't eat sushi yet | "yet" で「まだ」のニュアンス。日記では短縮形 can't が自然。"my daughter" の方が自然 |
| but I ordered | so I ordered | 「食べられない **から** 頼んだ」なので but ではなく so |
| cut small pieces so that she can eat | cut it into small pieces so she could eat it | **cut A into small pieces** の形。過去の話なので can → **could**(時制の一致)。eat の目的語 it も必要 |
| When my she ate the Udon | When she tasted the udon | "my" は消し忘れ。初めて口にした場面なら "tasted" がぴったり。udon は普通名詞なので小文字でOK |
| (顔を顰めた) | she made a face | **make a face** =「顔をしかめる/変な顔をする」。他に "she frowned"、"she grimaced"(より強い) |
| After finishing the lunch | After lunch | 食事名には通常 the を付けない。"After lunch" だけで十分 |
| a baby shop close to the sushi restaurant | a baby store near the restaurant | "near" の方が簡潔。アメリカ英語では store が一般的(shop も可) |
| My parents some cloths and toys for my baby | My parents bought her some clothes and toys | 動詞 **bought** が抜けている。**cloths(布) ≠ clothes(服)** に注意。"buy 人 物" の形が自然 |

**今日の表現**
- *come over (to my place)* — 家に遊びに来る
- *go out for sushi* — 寿司を食べに行く
- *make a face* — 顔をしかめる
- *one of the + 最上級 + 複数名詞*
